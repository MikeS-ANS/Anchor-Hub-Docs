# v3.11.2 — Contract Review: part-time seats & MRR auto-log

Six fixes to the User Audit Report's **Contract Review** tab and **Increase** flow, all from a live review session.

## Part-time users no longer look like a contract overage

The MSC workbook now has an **Included Part Time Users** column, and the audit counts part-time users separately from full-time ones.

Everything that drives a decision is now **full-time seats only** — the Over/Under figure, whether a client reads as CONFIRMED or PROVISIONAL, whether the Increase button appears, Step 1's price calculation, the Autotask push, and the MSC write-back.

Part-time users are still tracked and still visible:

- **Contracted** and **Supported** show as `7 + 2` — full-time, then part-time — on both the Account Manager Dashboard and Contract Review. A client with no part-time users looks exactly as it did before.
- A **PT +N** badge appears next to a client's name when you're supporting more part-time users than the contract includes. That's a prompt to have a contract conversation — it never changes the client's status, the Increase flow, or the billing digest.

This fixed a real false positive: a client who was within contract on full-time seats was showing up as needing an increase.

> The part-time column is new and still mostly blank. Until it's filled in, a client can show Contracted as a plain number while Supported reads `7 + 2`, with the PT badge showing. That's expected.

## Completed increases log themselves to the MRR workbook

As soon as Step 3 (the MSC sheet update) succeeds, the Hub writes one row into **MRR Contract Changes** — company, seat delta, the new per-user price, the effective date, and your increase note.

**Which quarter it lands in follows Step 2's effective date, not the day you make the push.** A change you key in March that takes effect 1 April is Q2 revenue and goes into Q2 — matching the date printed on the row itself. The quarter dropdown next to the effective date shows that quarter pre-selected and labelled with its year; override it when you're logging a genuine late entry.

If the log write fails, the MSC update still stands. You'll get a separate warning telling you to finish that one row by hand — the increase itself is never rolled back or reported as failed.

New contracts and cancellations are still entered by hand. Only existing-client seat increases are logged automatically.

> **Before the December/January run:** an increase effective in January belongs to the *next* year's tab. If that tab hasn't been created in the workbook yet, the log write stops and tells you which tab to create — it won't quietly drop the row into a closed quarter. Creating next year's tab ahead of time avoids this.

## Smaller fixes

- **Step 1 now requires New User Count.** It used to be labelled optional, and leaving it blank silently fell back to the audited supported-user figure. All three fields are required before you can save.
- **A client you've already dispositioned drops out of Confirmed Positives** — and out of the billing digest. *No change (within contract)*, *Waive / grandfather*, and *Increase contract (handled manually)* all stop showing as actionable. *Needs follow-up* still shows. This resets on its own when the next audit cycle starts, so a client still over contract comes back then.
- **Contract Review opens on Confirmed positives** every time you enter the tab, rather than All clients. Changing the dropdown still works normally, and Reload keeps whatever you've got selected.
- **Open MSC Sheet** and **Open MRR Sheet** buttons open either source workbook straight from Contract Review. They can take up to a minute to resolve, so they show a waiting state while they work.
