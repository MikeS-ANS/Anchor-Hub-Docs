# v3.9.1 — Fractional IT Leadership Report

**Released:** August 2026

## What's New

### A second report type inside Block Hour Report: Fractional IT Leadership

Block Hour Report's Dashboard and Review Queue were built around ANS's usual prepaid-support-hours clients — but a small handful of clients (Mountaingate Capital, Platte River Equity, and Michaud Capital Management) are on a different kind of engagement: **Fractional IT Leadership Services**, ongoing executive/IT leadership work rather than ad-hoc ticket-driven support. That doesn't fit the AI-drafted, review-and-approve flow the rest of the tool uses, so it now gets its own tab.

**New "Fractional IT Leadership" tab**

- Lists the Fractional clients the same way the main Dashboard does — hours purchased/used/remaining, usage bar, block expiration, last-sent date
- Click a client to see **Hours by Task** (billed work grouped by Autotask billing category, broken down by month for the current block) and **Hours by Block** (the client's full purchase history, every block ever bought)
- There's no AI-generated narrative here at all — just one fixed intro paragraph and numbers computed straight from Autotask, so there's nothing to draft or review
- A **live preview** shows exactly what would be emailed, built the same way the real send builds it, so there's no gap between what you see and what goes out — and it's laid out to render correctly in classic Outlook desktop, not just modern mail clients
- Click **Send** to email the client directly via Microsoft Graph — no queue, no approval step, since there's no editable text involved. If a live Autotask pull fails partway through, the tool refuses to send rather than risk a confidently-wrong "no hours logged" report reaching a client

**Fractional sends now show up in the main Send History tab**

A Fractional report send used to only show as a "last sent" date on that client's own row. It's now merged into Block Hour Report's existing Send History tab alongside every AI-narrative send, so there's one place to see everything that's gone out to any Block Hour client.

**New: Auto-send on the 1st**

Each Fractional client can now be opted in to a monthly auto-send — check the box on that client's row and their report goes out automatically on the 1st of the month (same schedule setting as the tool's other monthly generation), with no one needing to click Send by hand. It's off by default per client, since unlike the AI-narrative flow there's no human review step before a report goes out this way. A client with auto-send on that's manually sent mid-month, or vice versa, can't double-send — both paths share the same guard.
