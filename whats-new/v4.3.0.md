# v4.3.0 — Inventory Reports

Released 8 September 2026.

One new tool, for Accounting.

## Inventory Reports

The three monthly Autotask inventory reports Accounting reconciles — **On Hand**, **Added**, and **Picked** — now live in the Hub, with the month-end close built in. Until now they were LiveReports exported to Excel by hand on the 1st, reconciled against QuickBooks inside the spreadsheets. The full guide is under Tool Guides → [Inventory Reports](../tool-guides/inventory-reports.md).

**The reports, live**

- Pick a month; the three tabs read straight from Autotask with the same filters the old reports used. Per-unit cost and extended cost are shown separately and labelled correctly (the old export's "Unit Cost" column was really the extended amount).
- Picked shows one row per serialized unit, exactly as the legacy report did, and matches each charge to what actually left stock — exactly for serialized items, by product and timing for lots. Anything that does not line up sits in a **Needs review** strip under the table instead of quietly disappearing.
- Export to Excel in the legacy layout and file names, with real SUM formulas and the same precision Autotask holds.
- Settings (gear): the locations excluded from On Hand and the lot-match window.

**The month-end close**

- A **month-end snapshot** is taken automatically at 6:00 AM Denver on the 1st for the month that just closed, retrying every hour through that day. A snapshotted month shows exactly what it closed at, every time you open it. Settings has **Snapshot now** for a missed month and a confirmed **Replace** for a redo; a snapshot taken after the 1st is labelled late.
- Every row has a **Checked** box and a **Note**. Checked shows your initials; hover for who and when. Notes save when you leave the field, with "Saving…", "Saved", or "Not saved · Retry" shown beside them, and your text is never lost. Both export as the last two columns.
- Each tab has a **QB total** field: enter the QuickBooks figure and the Hub shows the variance (Autotask minus QuickBooks), green at zero.
- The **Summary** tab is the roll-forward: prior month-end On Hand, plus Added, minus Picked, minus Removed, minus stock moved into excluded locations, equals what should be on the shelf — against what actually is, with the unexplained variance stated plainly. Six drill-downs list the rows most likely to explain a difference, and a Reconciled block shows how many rows are checked.
- A third Picked match, **by product/units moved**, counts a lot's earlier same-month pick once two consecutive month-end snapshots exist.

Who sees it: finance and admin roles, managed in Access Management.

**First real check:** the automatic snapshot for September lands at 6:05 AM Denver on 1 October 2026. Accounting runs this alongside the old exports for one close before those are retired.
