# v3.4.2 — Meraki single-client refresh

## New

**Single-client refresh** — the Meraki License Management tool no longer requires a full "Re-scan All" (70+ orgs, can take a while) just to check one thing:

- **↻ Refresh button** — every org card now has one, next to **+ License** and **🎫 Ticket**. Rescans just that org, in place, without touching anyone else's data or blocking the page.
- **"Refresh one client" picker** — next to **Expand all / Collapse all**, for refreshing an org that isn't currently showing as a row (hidden by an active filter or search).
- Single-client refreshes get their own **Audit Log** entry ("Client refreshed"), separate from full scans.

See the [Meraki License & EoL Expiration Report guide](../tool-guides/meraki-expiration.md) for details, including the same shared-cache-file limitation that already applies to assigning unused licenses or syncing an AT warranty date.

## Fix

**Shelf-spare devices no longer inflate the summary counts** — unclaimed inventory devices with no license assigned were being counted as "Expired" in the top summary banner and Severity filter chips (they have no license expiry by design), which skewed the numbers. They're now excluded from those counts, matching how the HTML export already treated them. They're still visible under the **Status → Shelf spare** filter.

See [Roadmap](../getting-started/roadmap.md) for where this sits alongside the rest of the Hub.
