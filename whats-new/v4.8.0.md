# v4.8.0 — The Help Center comes into the Hub

Released 1 October 2026.

## Help opens right inside the Hub

Click **Help** in the sidebar and this Help Center now opens inside the Hub window. You don't need a browser any more. It replaces the old list of tool cards and the FAQ, so what you read in the Hub is always the current, published guide.

- **Back, forward and home arrows** at the top move around the guides, and the **open in browser** button opens the page you're on in your web browser.
- **Request Support** and **Report a Bug** are at the top of the Help screen, next to the app version and the download button for the latest installer.
- **If the Help Center can't load**, for example when you're offline, you'll see a short message with **Try again** and **Open in browser**, never a blank space. See [Troubleshooting](../getting-started/troubleshooting.md).

## A "?" for the tool you're in

While you're in a tool, a round **?** sits at the top right of the window, just left of your name. Click it, or Tab to it and press Enter, to open that tool's own guide straight away. It shows on every tool that has a guide, and hides on Home, the tools list and Help itself.

This was Mike Stewart's idea.
