# v4.10.0 — Client Map

Released 9 October 2026.

Every active Autotask customer, on a map. Susan Castle's idea (Hub idea #50).

## Add it to your sidebar first

Client Map is new, so it is not in anyone's sidebar yet. Open **Sidebar Layout** at the bottom of the sidebar, tick **Client Map**, and click **Save Layout**. It appears in your sidebar straight away.

## What you get

- **One pin per site**, coloured by how long it has been since that client was last touched: green on track, amber due soon, red overdue or never touched, grey for plans the Hub doesn't track. Same rule as Client Touch Aging, so the two never disagree.
- **Open tickets** as a number badge on the pin, and a **blue ring** when a dispatch visit is booked this week.
- **Quick views** for Denver metro, Colorado & Kansas, and All clients, plus plan-type chips, an account-manager filter, three toggles (Overdue for a touch, Has open tickets, Visit this week) and a search box. Your filters and quick view are remembered per person.
- **Night or Day.** A switch under the quick views changes the map between a dark Night style and a full-colour Day street map. Night is the default; your choice is remembered.
- **The client card.** Click a pin for the plan type, every site with a switcher, the address, last touched, account manager, contracted seats, the open-ticket list, upcoming dispatch visits for the next 14 days, and the last on-site visit. Buttons: **Add to visit day**, **Copy address**, **Open in Autotask**.
- **Visit day.** Add up to 10 clients from their cards and the Hub suggests an order from the ANS office with typical drive times for each leg and a day total. Drag to reorder, end at the last stop instead of returning, pick a date to see that day's dispatch visits faintly on the map, then **Copy route** or **Print**. Nothing is saved; a start address you type is used once and never kept.
- **The "couldn't place" list** tells you which site addresses need fixing in Autotask and why.

Client Map is **read-only**. Companies come only from Autotask: if a company isn't a Customer there, it isn't on the map.
