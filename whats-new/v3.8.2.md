# v3.8.2 — Hotfix: Sign-In Was Broken for New Sign-Ins

**Released:** August 2026

## What happened

A bug introduced in v3.8.1 could prevent the "Sign in with Microsoft" button from doing anything — clicking it would say **Opening browser for sign-in…** and then just sit there, with no browser window ever appearing.

This only affected people who actually needed to sign in fresh — a new install, or a session that had expired. If you were already signed in when v3.8.1 landed, you wouldn't have seen this at all, which is why it slipped through before release.

## What's fixed

Sign-in works normally again. If you hit this on v3.8.1, updating to v3.8.2 (or reinstalling from the usual link if the auto-updater can't reach you) resolves it — no other steps needed, and nothing about your account or settings is affected.

## Nothing else changed

This release only touches the sign-in bug. See [v3.8.1](v3.8.1.md) for the Timesheet Review Automatic Refresh feature it shipped alongside.
