# v3.12.0 — Payroll Processing

**Released:** August 2026

## What's New

### Payroll Processing (Beta)

The semi-monthly payroll spreadsheet, replaced. Department managers fill in their own people's changes in the Hub, Heather and Mike review everything in one place, and the finished period goes to Puzzle as a generated file instead of a hand-maintained one.

**Periods and the roster**

- Semi-monthly periods are created automatically, a few days before each one starts — nobody has to remember to open one
- Each period's roster comes from the same Paylocity export Payroll Review already reads, so employees and departments arrive on their own
- Every employee has one responsible manager: their department's manager by default, or a specific person where somebody owns an individual outside their department

**Manager entry**

- Each manager sees only their own people, with a row per employee for on-call, performance bonus, commission, regular hours, rewards crew and notes
- **Mileage and expenses are not typed in.** They are read from Autotask and are deliberately read-only in the grid, so the two figures most likely to be transcribed wrongly come straight from the source
- One sign-off per manager per period — a single "I've completed my updates", not a box per employee
- A payroll admin can sign off **on a manager's behalf** when somebody is away, which used to block the whole period. The manager is told it happened, by whom, and whether a reason was recorded

**Review and send**

- Heather and Mike see every department at once, grouped by the manager who actually owns each employee, with per-manager sign-off status
- Any value can be corrected directly on the review screen — no kicking it back and waiting
- **Approve & Send** shows exactly what will go out before anything happens: the Puzzle file with its row count, the recipients, and the editable email. A period can be reopened if something needs to change after sending

**Expense reports that need marking Paid**

Autotask's API will not let any application set an expense report to Paid — it accepts the request, reports success, and changes nothing. Rather than pretend otherwise, the Hub tells a human exactly which reports to flip by hand, in two places that cannot both be missed: an action list emailed the moment a period is sent, and a standing panel on the review screen. It then **re-reads each report's live status in Autotask** and clears them itself once they have actually moved. A report whose payment could never be confirmed is kept visibly separate from one that genuinely had nothing owed.

**Teams reminders**

Anchor Hub can now message people directly in Teams about the two things this tool asks them to remember:

- A **heads-up** two business days before the sign-off deadline
- **Escalating reminders** once that deadline passes — 8am, noon and 4pm on a business day, once a day at weekends — stopping the moment they sign off or the period closes
- A **Monday digest** to payroll admins of expense reports still waiting to be marked Paid
- A note to a manager when somebody **signed off on their behalf**

They arrive as a direct message from the **Anchor Hub** app in Teams, and they carry no dollar amounts and no employee names — a Teams message travels further than a screen inside the Hub does. **Reminders ship switched off** and are turned on deliberately from Review & Send, because a system that messages colleagues on a timer should not start doing it the moment it installs. If a reminder ever reaches nobody — someone hasn't got the Teams app, or has blocked it — that shows on the review screen as soon as it opens, rather than failing quietly.

**Access**

Gated on its own dedicated roles rather than the general Role Matrix: `hub.payroll` for Heather and Mike, and `hub.payrollmanager` for department managers, who only ever see their own people.
