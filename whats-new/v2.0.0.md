# v2.0.0 — HR & Resources Portal with Hatz AI

## HR & Resources Portal

A full self-service HR portal is now available in Anchor Hub. Access it from the left-hand navigation under **HR & Resources**.

### Quick Portal Tiles

One-click launch buttons for the five key HR platforms:

| Portal | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Paylocity** | Paystubs, time-off requests, tax documents |
| **Employee Navigator** | Benefits enrollment, life events, plan details |
| **Voya Financial** | 401(k), retirement planning, investments |
| **PuzzlePerks** | Employee discounts and perks marketplace |
| **OneDigital Financial Advisor** | Free personal financial advisor sessions |

Admins can override any portal URL from Edit mode to point to a company-specific login page.

### Benefit Cards

Flip cards showing the most important details for each benefit plan:

- **Medical** — carrier, plan name, group number, support phone
- **Dental** — carrier, group number, support phone
- **Vision** — carrier, group number, support phone
- **401(k)** — provider, plan name, advisor contact
- **Life Insurance** — provider, group number
- **Short & Long-Term Disability** — provider, group number

All field labels and values are editable by admins. Cards can be hidden, reordered, or custom cards can be added.

### HR Contacts

A configurable directory of HR contacts (internal staff + benefits contacts). Each entry shows name, title, email, and phone.

### What's New Banner

Admins can post announcements directly in the portal — plan changes, open enrollment reminders, or company updates. Leave it blank to hide the banner.

### Key Dates

Configurable reference dates for open enrollment windows, benefits deadlines, or any other dates employees should know.

### Hatz AI — "Ask HR AI"

A floating chat button in the bottom-right corner of the HR portal opens a conversational AI assistant powered by the **Anchor HR Guide** agent in Hatz AI. The agent has access to ANS-specific documents including:

- ANS Employee Handbook
- Benefit plan summaries
- Open enrollment guides

Employees can ask natural-language questions like "What holidays do we get?" or "How do I enroll a dependent?" and receive answers grounded in actual ANS policy documents. Conversation history persists across sessions (up to 40 messages).

## Admin Setup

HR portal content is stored in Azure Key Vault (`hr-portal-config` secret). The Hatz API key is stored in Key Vault as `hatz-api-key`.

To configure:
1. Navigate to **HR & Resources**
2. Click **Edit** (admin role required)
3. Fill in portal URLs, benefit card details, contacts, links, and dates
4. Click **Save changes**

The Hatz AI agent is managed separately in the Hatz AI console — upload plan documents and employee handbook PDFs there to keep the agent's knowledge current.
