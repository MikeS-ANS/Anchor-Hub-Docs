# v4.8.1 — Pop-ups that answer the keyboard, and payroll totals

Released 5 October 2026.

## Pop-up boxes work from the keyboard

The Hub's own pop-up boxes are the "OK" messages and the "OK / Cancel" questions you see across many tools. They now take the keyboard as soon as they open:

- **Enter** presses OK, or whichever button you've tabbed to.
- **Escape** closes the message, or answers Cancel to a question.
- **Tab** moves between the box's buttons and stays inside the box.

Before this, you had to click these boxes with the mouse. Pressing Enter while one was open could even press a button on the screen behind it. That can't happen any more: while a box is open, your keys only go to the box.

This fix came from a bug Mike Stewart reported (Hub bug #63).

## Payroll Processing: a Total row on both screens

The manager entry screen and the Review & Send grid now end in a **Total** row. It adds up Mileage, Expenses, On Call, Performance Bonus, CrewHu Rewards, Commission and Regular Hours for everyone on the screen. The total updates when you tab out of a cell you've changed.

- Mileage and Expenses that still say "not synced" count as zero.
- If a cell holds something that isn't a valid number, it's left out of the total. That total shows a `*`, and hovering it tells you how many values were skipped.
- The file sent to Puzzle hasn't changed.

See [Payroll Processing](../tool-guides/payroll-processing.md). The Total row was Chris Heck's idea (Hub idea #49).
