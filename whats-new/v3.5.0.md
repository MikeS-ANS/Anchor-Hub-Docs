# v3.5.0 — Strety Rocks & To-Dos homepage widget

## New

**My Rocks & To-Dos** — a new personal, read-only widget on the Home screen pulling your own open Strety Rocks and To-Dos, so you can see where you stand without leaving the Hub or opening a separate tab.

- **Connect Strety** — the widget starts dimmed with a "Connect Strety" prompt. Clicking it opens your browser to Strety's sign-in (a loopback OAuth flow, the same pattern used elsewhere in the Hub) and links your account once you approve access. The connection is per-user; your refresh token is stored in Azure Key Vault, not on your machine.
- **Rock counts** — On track / At risk / Off track buckets, based on your rocks' current status in Strety (not a due-date filter — Strety rocks routinely sit past their nominal due date without being closed out, so "assigned and not completed/cancelled" is what actually reflects real workload).
- **To-dos** — your open to-do count, plus your next 5 by due date.
- **Open in Strety** link jumps straight to strety.com for anything you need to act on.
- Matched to your Hub account by email — if no Strety profile matches, the widget says so plainly instead of showing an empty list that looks the same as "nothing assigned."

The Home screen's widget row now shows two columns side by side (Calendar + Rocks & To-Dos) instead of one full-width widget, so both fit above the fold.

This is a Home-screen widget, not a standalone sidebar tool — same category as the existing Calendar and Quick Links widgets — so there's no separate tool guide page for it. No AI involved; it's a direct read from Strety's API.
