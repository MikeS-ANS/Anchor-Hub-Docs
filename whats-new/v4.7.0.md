# v4.7.0 — Who is Where, and the full Today's Calendar

Released 30 September 2026.

## Who is Where

A new full-width widget on **Home** shows who is on-site with a client, pulled from Autotask service calls. Switch between **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **This Week** and **Next Week**, or open the **Calendar** for a whole month at a glance. It uses the same schedule as the weekday **Who is Where Dispatch** post in Teams.

- **Click a visit to open it in Autotask.** A visit tied to a ticket opens that ticket. A visit tied to a project task opens the task itself, not the project.
- **Untyped visits are shown separately.** A service call that isn't marked Onsite or Remote appears in its own group, as a reminder to set the type when dispatching.
- **It never shows an empty day it didn't actually confirm.** If Autotask can't be read, the widget says **"On-site schedule unavailable."** instead of "No on-sites today."

See [Who is Where (Daily On-Site Briefing)](../tool-guides/daily-onsite-briefing.md) for the full guide.

This was Susan Castle's idea.

## Today's Calendar shows every meeting

**Today's Calendar** on Home used to stop at six meetings, with no way to see the rest. It now lists every meeting you have today. On a busy day the list scrolls inside the widget once there are more than about six, so the rest of Home stays where it is.

Reported by Andi Gingerich.

## QuickBooks: groundwork only

This release also includes the Hub's QuickBooks Online connection, but it is still pointed at Intuit's **test company** while the real connection waits on Intuit's approval.

- In **Inventory Reports** you may see a greyed-out **Get from QuickBooks (test company)** button next to the QB total. It can't be clicked yet and doesn't change anything. Keep typing the QB total as usual.
- The button switches to the real books once the production connection is set up.

See [QuickBooks Connection](../tool-guides/quickbooks-connection.md) for details. This was Mike Stewart's idea.
