# v4.2.0 — Ideas & Bugs notifications, Timesheet Work Type, User Audit contact write-back

Released 4 September 2026.

Three new things and one fix.

## Ideas & Bug Tracker — Follow, and Teams notifications

Two gaps closed at once. A brand-new idea or bug used to notify nobody, so the only way to find out people were posting was to go and look. And commenting on something signed you up permanently, with no way off.

- **⚙ Notify me**, next to the bell, turns on a **Teams message** from the Hub bot when someone posts something new, replies to an item you follow, or changes its status. **Both settings are off for everyone until you switch them on yourself** — nobody is opted in by default, including admins, and nobody can opt you in.
- **Follow**, under any item's status, works both ways: follow something you had nothing to do with, or click again to **stop** updates on a thread that's gone noisy. Muting holds — it beats being the submitter, beats being merged in, beats having commented — and it survives a merge, so nobody's cleanup can quietly re-subscribe you.

You are never notified about your own actions. Replies to items you follow still appear in your bell whether the Teams setting is on or off; what the setting controls is the Teams message.

## Timesheet Review — Work Type in the Activity column

Ticket and project-task rows now show the entry's Autotask **Work Type** — Remote Support, Onsite Support, Triage, Travel Time, Correspondence, and so on. Those rows previously showed nothing but a generic "Service Ticket" or "Project Task" placeholder, which told you nothing about what the time was actually spent on.

General time entries are unchanged and still show their allocation code name. Work Type is display-only: it never moves an entry between buckets and never changes the client-facing %.

If a row still shows the old placeholder, it's coming from a scan cached before this release — hit **↻ Refresh** to pull it again.

## User Audit Report — Title, Phone and VIP write back to Autotask

Write-back used to act only on the support option. Worse than that: a returned row whose support option was *unchanged* was thrown away before anything else on it was read. So a client who corrected twenty job titles and left the dropdowns alone appeared to have returned no changes at all — those fields weren't merely unwritten, they were never even looked at.

All four client-editable columns are now compared and written:

- **The phone goes back to whichever field it came from**, mobile or main, so a correction can't leave a stale number sitting in the other one.
- **A blank cell never means "delete this."** If a client clears a title, it's left alone — emptying a field in Autotask stays a manual action.
- **Reformatting isn't a change.** `(303) 555-1234` and `303-555-1234` are the same number.
- **Autotask caps titles at 50 characters and phone numbers at 25.** Anything longer is flagged in the preview *before* you confirm, and reported as skipped rather than written — a truncated job title or phone number *looks* correct in the PSA, which is worse than one that was never written.
- **A row where only the title changed writes only that title.** It never touches the support option, and it cannot deactivate anyone.
- **An unanswered dropdown no longer throws the row away.** If a client fixes someone's title but leaves their support option blank, the title still gets written.

The preview lists each changed field on its own line, with the support option called out in orange, so you can tell at a glance whether a batch is re-classifying people or just tidying up job titles.

**This applies to audit sheets generated from v4.2.0 onward.** Audits already sitting in clients' inboxes were built without the hidden baseline values this compares against; those returns keep working exactly as they do today, support option only.

## Fixed

- **Strety kept asking you to reconnect** (bug #32). The "needs reconnect" prompt no longer depends on a Key Vault write succeeding — which only admins can do — so anyone else stops being asked to reconnect a connection that was never broken.
- **Submitting an idea or comment could look like it failed when it hadn't.** The Teams notification runs as part of the submit, and a slow one could outlast the app's wait, reporting a failure for something that had actually saved. Clicking Submit again then produced a duplicate item and a second round of messages.
