# v4.3.1 — Auto-Start and Minimize to Tray

Released 8 September 2026.

One change to how the app runs in the background.

## Auto-start at Windows login, minimize to tray

Anchor Hub now launches automatically when you sign in to Windows and shows itself on screen right away — no need to find it in the Start Menu.

The bigger change is what happens when you close it. Closing the window — the titlebar's **X**, Alt+F4, or closing it from the taskbar — no longer quits the app. It minimizes to a new **system tray icon** instead, and keeps running in the background.

- **Open Anchor Hub**, from the tray icon, brings the window back
- **Quit Anchor Hub**, from the tray icon's right-click menu, is now the only way to actually exit
- The first time a close minimizes instead of quitting, a one-time tray notification explains what just happened, so it doesn't read as the app hanging
- An update installing, or choosing **Quit Anchor Hub** from the tray, still exits the app normally

Auto-start is registered every time you launch a packaged build, so it self-heals on its own — there's nothing to configure, and nothing that can silently get switched off.
