# HR & Resources Portal

The HR & Resources portal gives Anchor employees quick access to benefit portals, plan information, HR contacts, and an AI assistant for benefits questions — all from within Anchor Hub.

## Accessing the Portal

Click **HR & Resources** in the left-hand navigation bar.

## Quick Portal Tiles

The top section of the portal shows five tiles that open benefit platforms in your default browser:

- **Paylocity** — Paystubs, time-off requests, W-2s, direct deposit settings
- **Employee Navigator** — Benefits enrollment, plan details, life event changes
- **Voya Financial** — 401(k) balance, contribution rate, investment options
- **PuzzlePerks** — Employee discount marketplace
- **OneDigital Financial Advisor** — Free personal financial advisor (virtual or in-person)

Click any tile to open that platform. Admins can update the URL for any tile from Edit mode.

## Benefit Cards

Below the portal tiles, benefit cards show key plan details for each coverage type. Each card has a front and back:

- **Front** — Carrier logo, plan name, and quick-reference fields (group number, support phone, etc.)
- **Back (edit mode)** — Editable fields for admins to enter or update plan details

Visible cards are controlled by your admin. Cards can be hidden if a benefit doesn't apply to your company.

## HR Contacts

The Contacts section lists internal HR staff and benefits contacts with name, title, email, and phone number. Click an email address to open your mail client.

## What's New

If your HR admin has posted an announcement (open enrollment dates, plan changes, policy reminders), it appears in a banner at the top of the portal.

## Key Dates

Important HR dates (open enrollment windows, benefit deadlines) are listed in the sidebar.

## Ask HR AI

A purple **Ask HR AI** button floats in the bottom-right corner of the portal. Click it to open a chat panel where you can ask benefits questions in plain language.

**Examples:**
- "What holidays do we get off?"
- "How do I add a dependent to my health plan?"
- "What's our dental group number?"
- "How much does ANS contribute to the 401k?"

The AI assistant is powered by the Anchor HR Guide agent in Hatz AI, which has been trained on ANS-specific documents including the employee handbook and benefit plan summaries. For questions it can't answer from the uploaded documents, it will say so rather than guessing.

Chat history persists between sessions so you can scroll back to previous answers. Use the trash icon to clear history.

## For Admins

### Editing Portal Content

Click the **Edit** button (visible to admin users only) to enter edit mode. From here you can:

- Override portal URLs for any of the five tiles
- Update benefit card field values and labels
- Add, remove, reorder, or hide benefit cards
- Add or edit HR contacts
- Add or remove quick links
- Set key dates
- Post a "What's New" announcement

Click **Save changes** to persist all edits to Azure Key Vault. Changes are visible immediately after saving.

### Keeping the AI Agent Current

The AI assistant answers questions based on documents uploaded to the Hatz AI console. During open enrollment or after plan changes:

1. Log in to the Hatz AI console
2. Find the **Anchor HR Guide** agent
3. Upload updated benefit summaries, plan documents, or handbook revisions
4. The agent immediately uses the new documents for all future questions

The agent supports up to 20 uploaded files. Remove outdated documents when uploading updated versions.
